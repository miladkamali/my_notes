#+title: GBU-57A/B MOP

* GBU-57A/B Massive Ordnance Penetrator (MOP)
** Overview
The GBU-57A/B is a precision-guided "bunker buster" developed by Boeing for the United States Air Force. It is designed to accomplish the difficult mission of destroying adversaries' weapons of mass destruction located in well-protected, deep underground facilities.

** Penetration
- Up to 200 feet (61 m) of unspecified material or earth.
- Up to 60 feet (18.3 m) of standard concrete, or 8 feet (2.4 m) into 10,000 psi (69 MPa) reinforced concrete.

** Payload & Specifications
- Class: 30,000-pound (13,600 kg) class bomb.
- Length: 20.5 feet (6.2 m).
- Diameter: 31.5 inches (0.8 m / 80 cm).
- Warhead: BLU-127 bomb body weighing 27,125 lb (12,304 kg).
- Explosive Weight: 5,342 lbs (2,423 kg) total, comprising 4,590 lbs (2,082 kg) of AFX-757 and 752 lbs (341 kg) of PBXN-114.
- High-density Eglin steel alloy casing engineered to survive extreme stresses of deep penetration.

** Usage & Guidance
- Delivery Platforms: Northrop B-2 Spirit and B-21 Raider.
- Guidance: Integrated GPS and Inertial Navigation System (INS).
- Control: Utilizes grid fins (instead of planar fins) which fold for storage in bomb bays and offer greater control at high speeds.
- Fuze: Large Penetrator Smart Fuze (LPSF) adjusts detonation timing based on impact depth and structural characteristics.

** Estimated Production
- Manufacturer: Boeing.
- Number Built: At least 20 units (USAF began receiving them in 2011, with 16 received by November 2011).

** Operational Usage
- June 22, 2025: First combat use during Operation Midnight Hammer. Seven B-2 stealth bombers dropped 14 GBU-57s on Iran's Fordow Uranium Enrichment Plant and Natanz Nuclear Facility.
- 2026 Iran War: Further utilized against Hard and Deeply Buried Targets (HDBTs).
