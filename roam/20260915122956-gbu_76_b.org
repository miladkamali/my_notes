#+title: GBU-76/B 

* GBU-76/B Next Generation Penetrator (NGP)
** Overview
The GBU-76/B, formally known as the Next Generation Penetrator (NGP), is a bunker-buster program currently in development by the U.S. Air Force to succeed the GBU-57 MOP. It is optimized for survivability, precision, and long-range bomber operations against deeply buried command centers and strategic infrastructure in GPS-contested environments.

** Penetration
- Specific capabilities remain classified, but it is intended to offer comparable or superior penetration against reinforced concrete and rock compared to the GBU-57 MOP.
- Capable of blast, fragmentation, and penetration effects.

** Payload & Specifications
- Class: 20,000 to 30,000-pound (9,070 to 13,600 kg) class weapon system.
- Warhead: Described in earlier notices as weighing 22,000 pounds (9,980 kg) or less.

** Usage & Guidance
- Delivery Platforms: Designed to be compatible with the B-21 Raider (and potentially other tactical platforms), making it slightly lighter and more versatile than the MOP.
- Guidance: Designed for high terminal accuracy (CE90 within 7.2 ft / 2.2 m) in "GPS-aided, degraded, and/or denied environments." Likely to incorporate Alternate Navigation Systems, anti-jam/anti-spoofing functions, and a multi-mode seeker.
- Fuze: Next-generation hard-target smart fuze that can "count" structural floors and sense underground "voids" to calculate the exact optimal detonation depth.
- Potential Additions: Exploring the integration of rocket boosters for standoff strike capabilities.

** Estimated Production
- Status: In early developmental/prototype stage. 
- A contract was awarded in September 2025 to Applied Research Associates and Boeing to build a prototype. Full prototype demonstrations are expected by the end of Fiscal Year 2027.

** Operational Usage
- Currently in development; no operational combat usage yet.
