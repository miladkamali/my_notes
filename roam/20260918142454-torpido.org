#+title: torpido
Overview
This document lists the torpedo systems and related anti-submarine warfare (ASW) concepts mentioned in the video "U.S. Navy’s GENIUS Upgrade Makes Torpedoes FLY."

Mentioned Torpedo Systems and Launch Platforms

* [[id:2c6adfcb-ce32-41fa-a7a6-663dc62102af][SAA-LT]] Silent Anvil Air-Launched Torpedo (SAA-LT)

Description: A stealth stand-off weapon designed to glide silently through the air before submerging to strike enemy submarines, significantly reducing acoustic detection windows.

Launch Platforms: BRU-61/A bomb racks on fighters, bombers, and heavy drones.

* [[id:486ec22c-d25f-4eb9-b804-6e7768d23ac4][Mark 48]] Mark 48 (Mk 48) Heavyweight Torpedo

Description: The primary heavyweight anti-submarine and anti-surface warfare torpedo in the U.S. Navy arsenal.

Launch Platforms: Submarine 21-inch torpedo tubes, and the newly conceptualized Containerized "Liberator" Seabed Launcher (as highlighted in the video).

* [[id:8776f847-9f7e-4ad4-8eeb-722990a2a4ef][MK54]] Mark 54 (Mk 54) Lightweight Torpedo

Description: The standard lightweight torpedo used for ASW by the U.S. Navy, facing modern proximity risks which are mitigated by stand-off launch concepts.

Launch Platforms: Surface ships (notably utilizing the LRAW Rocket Booster Range Extension mentioned in the video), maritime patrol aircraft, and ASW helicopters.
