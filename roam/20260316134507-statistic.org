** correlation vs causation
- correlation indicates a relationship or association between the two
- it doesn't imply one is the cause for the other
- positive correlation means two features move in the same direction
- negative correlation means two features move in the opposite directions


- causation implies one feature directly effect the other one
- it establishes a cause and effect relationship
- pearson correlation is not sufficient for determining causation, it requires rigorous testing and investigation
