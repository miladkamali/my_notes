#+title: sed

concating multiple line together into one line

sed 'N;N;s/\n/,/g' input.txt

How it works:

    N;N;: These two N commands pull the next two lines into the pattern space, so sed is holding three lines at once.

    s/\n/,/g: This replaces all internal newlines (the ones between the three lines) with commas.
