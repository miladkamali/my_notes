#+title: Mark 48
Overview
The Mark 48 and its improved Advanced Capability (ADCAP) variant are heavyweight acoustic-homing torpedoes designed to sink fast, deep-diving nuclear-powered submarines and high-performance surface ships.

Launch Platforms

Submarine Torpedo Tubes (standard 533 mm)

"Liberator" Containerized Seabed Launcher (experimental/concept platform mentioned in recent ASW developments)

Technical Specifications (Metric)

Mass: 1,676 kg

Length: 5.79 m (often rounded to 5.8 m)

Diameter: 533 mm

Warhead Mass: 292.5 kg (High Explosive)

Performance Metrics

Maximum Speed: Estimated at 102 km/h (55 knots)

Operational Range:

~38 km at 102 km/h (55 knots)

~50 km at 74 km/h (40 knots)

Maximum Operating Depth: Estimated at 800 m

Propulsion: Piston engine with pump jet, fueled by Otto fuel II.
