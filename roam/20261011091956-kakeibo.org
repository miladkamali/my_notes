#+title: kakeibo

the art of mindful spending

kakeibo helps you manage your finances by mindful budgeting at the beginning of
each month by deducting your constant expenditure from your income and setting
you with allocated budget you can spend. while doing so you set a realistic goal
for saving fixed amount of money.
