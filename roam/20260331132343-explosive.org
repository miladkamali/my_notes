#+title: explosive

* explosive classification
** Class A
substances that has a maximum hazard capability because of detonation
*** List
**** Dynamite
**** Lead azide
**** picric Acid
**** Mercury fulminate
** Class B
substances that poses a flammable hazard such as propellant explosive
*** list
**** smokeless propellant
**** photographic flash powder
**** 
** Class C
manufactured items that contain either class A or B or combination of them such as
detcord,  bullet , ...
** type of explosive
*** low explosive
rate of decomposition proceed through material at less than speed of sound,
basically deflagration 
*** High explosive
rate of decomposition proceed through material at super sonic speed, causing
detonation
** sensitivity of explosive
*** contact
very sensitive to stimuli,
nitrogen triiodide can explode by being touched by a feather
[[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DFfRqoIdArM][slow motion contac explosive nitrogen triiodide]]
*** primary
explosive that usually found in detonators,
lead azide, acetone peroxide  mercury (||) fulminate and nitrogrlycerin 
*** secondary
is less sensitive than primary explosive and requires substantially more energy
to initiate
TNT(Trinitrotoluene), RDX(1,2,5-Trinitroperhydro-1,3,5 triazine) and PETN (pentaerythritol Tetranitrate) 
*** tertiary
** type of stimuli for explosive to react to 
*** impact
*** heat
*** friction
*** Electromagnetic radiation
*** static electricity
