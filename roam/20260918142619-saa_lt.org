#+title: SAA-LT

* Overview
The Silent Anvil Air-Launched Torpedo (SAA-LT) is a next-generation U.S. Navy concept designed to extend the range of anti-submarine warfare (ASW) capabilities while keeping launch platforms safe from advanced air defenses. It acts as a stealth glide weapon, deploying a torpedo from high altitude and gliding to the target area before entering the water.

* Key Features

Stealth Glide: Glides silently through the air to minimize acoustic and radar detection.

Standoff Range: Keeps aircrews outside the threat range of modern anti-aircraft defenses.

Integration: Designed to integrate with existing standard bomb racks (BRU-61/A).

Launch Platforms

Strike Fighters

Bombers

Heavy Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (Drones)
